How Adjusted Performance works

Estimates top-cut performance using each event’s field and cut size, with small samples pulled toward the field baseline. A score of 100 is the baseline on a common mix of events. Higher is better; this is not a win percentage.

The range shows uncertainty under the model. Similar scores do not establish a clear difference. “Limited data” means fewer than five events or five identified pilots, or missing pilot IDs. Pilot skill and individual decklists are not isolated.

CR and MS retain their existing source definitions and three-month window. Performance uses finished cuts from events with complete commander labels in the window shown above, so its entry totals can differ.
